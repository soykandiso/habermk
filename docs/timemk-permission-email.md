# Email to Time.mk: permission to use the RSS feed

Why: Time.mk's terms forbid automated access and its robots.txt blocks `/r/`,
so we use vesti.mk instead. With written permission, Time.mk's RSS could be a
second "what is trending" signal. Nothing changes until they say yes.

Send to: the contact address on https://time.mk/info (company: ТАЈМ-БОРГ ДООЕЛ).
Fill in the parts in [brackets] (delete "некомерцијален" if you plan ads or
sponsors) and check the Macedonian before sending.
